How it works

Pick a deed. Do it. Enjoy your reward.

Open the mobile website on your phone, choose your travel dates and see what's available during your stay. No download, no sign-up.

1

Pick a good deed

Browse by area, type of deed or reward. Filter for families, accessibility or what's open now.

2

Start at the partner

Go to the partner and swipe to start. Scan the partner's QR code to confirm you're there. That takes two seconds and needs no personal data.

3

Show your screen

When you're done, show the confirmed reward on your phone and enjoy it. Then find the next one.

What makes it different

Built for Prague's real challenge: everyone in the same five streets

Rewards for green travel are a proven idea in other European cities. Praha Bonus Visitor adds features designed for a city where crowds gather in the centre while the rest of Prague is waiting to be discovered.

🧭NEW

Quieter-right-now nudges

When a hotspot is crowded, the site suggests a quieter place nearby and adds a bonus reward for going there.

🕘NEW

Off-peak bonuses

Extra rewards for visiting museums, viewpoints and landmarks early in the morning or on weekdays.

📍NEW

Simple QR check-in

A QR code at every partner confirms your visit. Fair for partners, still no account and no tracking for you.

🗺️NEW

Neighbourhood story trails

Self-guided walks with QR stops in Vyšehrad, Žižkov, Karlín, Holešovice and Letná, each ending with a local reward.

🧾NEW

Your impact postcard

At the end of your stay, get a shareable summary: car trips avoided, litter collected, neighbourhoods visited, local businesses supported.

👨‍👩‍👧NEW

Family and access filters

Find deeds suitable for children, step-free locations and quiet spaces, in English, Czech, German, Spanish, Italian and French.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No. Praha Bonus Visitor is a mobile website. Open it in your phone's browser, choose your travel dates and start. You don't need to give your name or email.

How do I prove I did the good deed?

Swipe to start, scan the QR code at the partner, and show the confirmed reward on your screen when you're done. Some partners also check, for example a bag of collected litter or a train ticket.

Is it free?

Yes. Taking part is free. Rewards are free items or discounts offered by partners.

Do you track my location?

No. The QR code confirms you're at the partner. Busyness levels come from anonymous visitor counts, never from your phone.

Why are there extra rewards outside the centre?

A few streets in the centre get very crowded while many great neighbourhoods are quiet. Bonus rewards spread visitors out, which means shorter queues for you and a better balance for residents.

Can I join with children?

Yes. Use the Families filter to find deeds and rewards suitable for children.

I live in Prague. Can I take part?

Residents have their own programme, Praha Bonus, with a points wallet for everyday good deeds. Visitors and residents share many of the same partners.

Who is behind it?

Praha Bonus Visitor is a concept for a programme run by an independent operator together with the city, its tourism partners and local businesses. All offers on this website are examples.
